HealthTech · Preventive cardiology

Cardisio

AI-supported heart screening that takes four minutes and delivers a report in under ten.

Cardisiography is a non-invasive procedure based on digital vectorcardiography. A short resting recording with five electrodes is analysed on a server and returns a structured report, including a guideline-compliant ECG. Designed for primary care physicians and cardiologists who want to detect coronary heart disease, atrial fibrillation and heart failure earlier.

  • 4-minute recording
  • Report in under 10 minutes
  • Published clinical study
  • Made in Germany

Future market: early detection and prevention in everyday medical practice.

Sustainable logistics · Circular packaging

Pacture

A reusable cover system that replaces stretch film on roll containers in food logistics.

Woven covers made from recycled polypropylene are pulled over the container and fastened with hook-and-loop strips. No machine, no power, no training. Custom sizes, logos, colour-coded edges, label pockets, QR codes or RFID chips are available, for purchase or leasing. Developed by Kupek GmbH in Upper Franconia and winner of the German Sustainability Award Products 2027 in the Resources category.

  • 12,000+ sets in use
  • 300+ uses per set
  • Up to 90 kg less film per trolley per year
  • German Sustainability Award 2027
pacture.de ↗

Future market: reusable systems in retail and food supply chains.

FinTech · Financial education

Investment Arena

A trading simulator with real market prices, where talent gets noticed.

Users start with 100,000 € of virtual capital and trade long and short on real prices, with an optional professional mode for options and leverage. Private and public leagues, copy-trading of community funds and monthly prizes make learning competitive. A talent score can lead to a scouting contact with the analyst team around financier Florian Homm. Everything is a learning simulation, not investment advice, and no real orders reach an exchange.

  • 100,000 € virtual capital
  • Real market prices
  • Leagues & monthly prizes
  • Talent scouting
investment-arena.com ↗

Future market: gamified financial education and community-driven investing.
